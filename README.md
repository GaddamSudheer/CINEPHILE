# CINEPHILE

### Discover Cinema Beyond Popularity

CINEPHILE is a movie and television series discovery and recommendation platform designed to help viewers explore cinema beyond conventional popularity-based recommendations.

The platform aims to surface critically acclaimed films, cult classics, hidden gems, international cinema, independent productions and overlooked masterpieces.

## Project Vision

Traditional recommendation systems often emphasize popularity, ratings and mainstream visibility.

CINEPHILE aims to provide a more diverse discovery experience by considering:

- Critical acclaim
- Cult status
- Cinematic influence
- Genre and thematic similarity
- International and regional cinema
- Hidden gems and overlooked films
- Individual viewing preferences
